# Bent Sheet Metal

The intiial plan for the SQUID-6 enclosure was a sleek bent sheet metal design with wooden side panels. While this option would have provided a solid and durable enclosure, unexpected cost increases due to factors like tariffs made this option too expensive for the initial design cycle.

While the bent sheet metal design wasn't realized in this initial design, this enclosure design would provide a higher-quality finish if the SQUID-6 is ever made at scale.

# Laser Cut Plywood

The current enclosure of the SQUID-6 is a laser-cut plywood design featuring laser engraved labelling.

# 3D Printed Knobs

Some of the knobs on the SQUID-6 are large, and available options are often expensive, so custom knobs were investigated to reduce costs and allow for a more custom design. Multiple 3D-printed concepts were designed and prototyped, but have not been fully realized yet. The design currently features non-custom knobs.
